- **DataTray tells you what it is doing while it starts.** A small window shows the real startup steps
  (applying staged plugin updates, loading your plugins, putting your workspace back, starting
  extensions) as rows filling a tray. A step that takes longer than five seconds says so, how long it has
  waited and why DataTray needs it; after thirty seconds on your plugins you can start without the ones
  from the Plugin Store. If a step fails you see the error and can retry, open the log, continue with the
  built-in engines or quit, instead of the app closing without a window. The splash follows your theme,
  shrinks to a compact layout on small screens and after a restart, and holds still when your system asks
  for reduced motion.
