- **Updating no longer loses your open tabs.** Installing an update closed DataTray without saving the
  session, so the new version started with empty or outdated tabs. The tabs, their text (also unsaved),
  the active tab and the window layout are now saved before the update installs, the same way as when
  you quit. The open tabs are also saved every 30 seconds while something changed, so a crash or a forced
  quit costs at most that.
