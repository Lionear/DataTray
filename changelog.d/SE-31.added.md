- **Star a connection to keep it within reach.** Right-click a connection → *Add to favorites*, and it
  also appears in a Favorites section pinned to the top of the sidebar, whatever folder it lives in. The
  connection itself is stored once — the section is a second view of it, so editing, renaming or deleting
  works the same wherever you do it. Temporary connections (created by an AI client over MCP) can't be
  starred, since they don't outlive the session.
