- **Scrollbars are always shown in full.** They used to be a thin overlay line that only widened into a
  grabbable bar after you hovered it for a moment, so a quick drag often missed. Now every scrollbar, from
  the result grid and SQL editor to the connection tree, lists, text fields and dialogs, is a full bar you
  can grab at once, with content beside it instead of underneath. Prefer the slimmer look? Turn on
  **Preferences › Appearance › Overlay scrollbars**. It applies live.
