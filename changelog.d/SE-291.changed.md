- **DataTray draws its own title bar.** The menu moved up into it, which gives the window back the row
  the separate menu bar used to take, and the bar itself now says which connection you are on and which
  document is in front of you — something the system title bar could only ever spell "DataTray". On
  macOS the traffic lights stay where they belong; elsewhere the minimise, maximise and close buttons
  are DataTray's own.
